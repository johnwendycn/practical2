PROMPT 4.1: INTRODUCING ASPECT-BASED SENTIMENT ANALYSIS
================================================================================

"Now we're going to perform ASPECT-BASED SENTIMENT ANALYSIS.

This is different from general sentiment analysis because:
- General sentiment: 'This is a negative comment'
- Aspect-based sentiment: 'The app speed is positive, but the customer service is negative'

Aspect-based sentiment analysis tells us:
- Which aspects customers are happy about
- Which aspects need improvement
- What drives positive and negative experiences

We'll analyze sentiment for EACH aspect separately."

================================================================================
PROMPT 4.2: COPILOT PROMPT FOR ASPECT-BASED SENTIMENT ANALYSIS
================================================================================

"Copy this EXACT prompt and paste it into Copilot:

'Now I want you to perform ASPECT-BASED SENTIMENT ANALYSIS.

For EACH aspect that you identified, analyze the sentiment of comments that mention that aspect.

Add THREE new columns:
1. aspect_sentiment_score - Sentiment score specifically for the aspect (from -1.0 to +1.0)
2. aspect_sentiment_category - Positive, Negative, or Neutral for the aspect
3. aspect_sentiment_explanation - Brief explanation of why this aspect received this sentiment

IMPORTANT: The sentiment should be SPECIFIC to the aspect, not the overall comment.

For example:
- Comment: 'The app is fast but customer service is slow'
  - Overall sentiment might be neutral
  - Aspect 'App Speed' → Positive
  - Aspect 'Customer Service' → Negative

For each aspect, identify:
- What words or phrases relate to that aspect
- Whether those words are positive or negative
- How strongly positive or negative they are

Process all rows. Focus on the aspect you identified for each comment.

Use this sentiment scoring scale:
- +1.0 to +0.6: Positive
- +0.5 to +0.1: Slightly Positive
- 0.0 to -0.1: Neutral
- -0.2 to -0.6: Slightly Negative
- -0.7 to -1.0: Negative

Consider Nigerian banking context and cultural nuances in your sentiment analysis.'

WAIT for Copilot to complete the analysis."
